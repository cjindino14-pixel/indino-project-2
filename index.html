Our Story

A legacy of faith and education

Founded in 1927, KCC has been part of the educational story of Kabankalan City for generations. We continue to form learners who are knowledgeable, responsible, compassionate, and ready to serve.

Our learning community brings together academic formation, Catholic values, student development, and service to others.
